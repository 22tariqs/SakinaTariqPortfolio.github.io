About Me

My work sits at the intersection of business operations, people development, and technology enablement. A technical foundation in informatics and data analytics grounds how I approach organizational problems — but the work itself has consistently pulled me toward operations, training, and change management: redesigning how teams work, building the programs that bring people along, and coordinating across stakeholders to get initiatives adopted.

Across internships at NYSTEC and NYSED, I've led learning and development initiatives reaching 1,000+ employees — including designing and building an AI Fundamentals training course from the ground up — redesigned project and workflow tracking in Asana to improve team coordination, partnered with stakeholders to scope process and technology changes, and supported risk, governance, and compliance programs across dozens of organizations. The common thread is operations: finding where a process, a team, or a system can run more effectively, and building the training, tools, or workflow to get it there.

Research

UAlbany CEHC · Minerva Center for High Impact Learning
From Coast to Coast: Comparing How Air Pollution Influences Asthma in Two States
Sakina Tariq

Used the Asthma Equity Explorer to compare PM2.5 concentrations and asthma prevalence across 62 New York counties and 58 California counties. Found particulate matter significantly (though weakly) associated with asthma prevalence in New York, while no significant relationship emerged in California — pointing to geography, terrain, and population density as areas for further study.

→ View research poster
UAlbany Showcase · College of Emergency Preparedness, Homeland Security and Cybersecurity
Digital Safety in the Modern Muslim World
Sakina Tariq & Jill Cofield

Explored how cybersecurity awareness education can improve digital safety among Muslim communities in the U.S., combining survey-based research, Protection Motivation Theory, and the CyBOK framework with a community-engaged, mixed-methods approach to design culturally relevant training.
